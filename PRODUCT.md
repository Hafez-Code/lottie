# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People creating Lottie animations on desktop with an AI coding agent. They describe a scene, supply visual references, inspect the rendered result, and request focused revisions without leaving their project.

## Product Purpose

Studio ProMax provides a continuous workflow from a brief to a rendered, editable animation. Projects and scenes retain their conversation and references. Agent execution must be visible, cancellable, and distinct from saving a brief.

## Brand Personality

Keep the existing application design, as requested by the user: restrained, familiar, practical. Reuse its typography, controls, colors, and spacing.

## Anti-references

Avoid introducing an unrelated visual identity or displaying simulated AI progress as successful execution.

## Design Principles

- Keep the conversation accessible while working on a scene.
- Distinguish discovery, configuration, execution, and verification.
- Ground revisions in the current scene and selected element.
- Preserve project work and explain recoverable failures.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use labeled native controls, visible keyboard focus, readable status text, and keyboard-accessible layer selection. No additional accessibility requirements were specified.
